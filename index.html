Before we talk

Useful
questions.

Do we need to replace all our current tools?

Usually not. We start with the tools and information you already have, keep what works and change only what prevents a clear or timely answer.

Will you only build a dashboard, or also explain the results?

The explanation is part of the value. The goal is to show what changed, why it may be happening, what deserves attention and which questions or actions should come next.

Do you offer one-off projects or ongoing support?

Both. A defined cleanup, dashboard or automation can be delivered as a project. Evolving analysis, maintenance and improvements are better suited to a monthly arrangement.

Does automation always need AI?

No. Many useful automations only need clear rules and connections between tools. AI is added when the task requires reading, sorting, summarising or interpreting information—and only when it improves the result.

What do you need for a first conversation?

A rough description is enough: what you want to understand or stop doing manually, how you handle it today and what a better result would look like.
