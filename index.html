Before we talk

Useful
questions.

Do we need a data warehouse or an expensive BI tool?

Not necessarily. The solution should match the decision, volume and team. Sometimes a well-designed Google Sheet is enough; sometimes connected BI or a small data model is justified.

Can you work with our existing spreadsheets and tools?

Yes. Replacing everything is rarely the right first move. I start by understanding what already works, where it breaks and which part creates the most avoidable effort.

Do you offer one-off projects or ongoing support?

Both. A defined cleanup, dashboard or automation can be delivered as a project. Evolving analysis, maintenance and improvements are better suited to a monthly arrangement.

Where does AI fit into the work?

Only where it creates a dependable advantage: classifying information, drafting report commentary, detecting patterns or supporting repetitive workflows. Critical outputs keep validation and human review.

What do you need for a first conversation?

A rough description is enough: what your team does manually, which tools are involved, how often it happens and what would improve if the problem disappeared.
