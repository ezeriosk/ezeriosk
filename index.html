Before we talk

Useful
questions.

Do we need a data warehouse or an expensive BI tool?

Not necessarily. The solution should match the decision, volume and team. Sometimes a well-designed Google Sheet is enough; sometimes connected BI or a small data model is justified.

Can you automate our existing spreadsheets and tools?

Usually, yes. Replacing everything is rarely the right first move. I connect what already works, strengthen the fragile parts and automate the repetitive handoffs around it.

Do you offer one-off projects or ongoing support?

Both. A defined cleanup, dashboard or automation can be delivered as a project. Evolving analysis, maintenance and improvements are better suited to a monthly arrangement.

How do you use AI without making the workflow unreliable?

AI handles tasks where some judgement helps—classification, extraction, commentary or investigation—but receives structured context, is evaluated against examples and keeps human approval for critical actions.

What do you need for a first conversation?

A rough description is enough: which decision or report repeats, what the team does manually, which tools are involved and what faster information would let you do differently.
